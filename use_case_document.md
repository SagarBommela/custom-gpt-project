Custom GPT Use Case Document
1. GPT Name

Employee Leave Policy Assistant

2. Target Users
Primary Users

Employees — Employees will use the GPT to get quick answers to common leave-policy questions based on approved policy documents.

Secondary Users

HR Staff / HR Support Personnel — HR staff may benefit by reducing the need to answer routine policy questions. HR remains responsible for questions requiring human review, exceptions, or final decisions.

3. Problem Statement

Employees need quick access to accurate information about common leave-policy questions, including leave types, eligibility, and approval procedures. The proposed GPT will provide guidance based on approved policy documents while directing questions that require human review or fall outside the documented scope to HR.
4. Expected Outcomes

| # | Expected Outcome       | Proposed Goal                                                                                                  | Measurement                                                    |
| - | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | Faster responses       | **Goal:** 90% of supported questions receive an initial response within 10 seconds.                            | Measure response time during pilot testing.                    |
| 2 | Knowledge coverage     | **Goal:** Correctly address at least 90% of predefined common leave-policy questions.                          | Test representative questions against approved policy content. |
| 3 | Answer accuracy        | **Goal:** At least 95% of evaluated responses accurately reflect approved policy documents.                    | Compare responses with approved source documents.              |
| 4 | Pilot adoption         | **Goal:** At least 70% of participating employees use the GPT during the pilot period.                         | Track participating users who submit at least one question.    |
| 5 | Appropriate escalation | **Goal:** At least 95% of out-of-scope test questions are correctly identified for clarification or HR review. | Test out-of-scope and exception scenarios.                     |

5. Required Knowledge and Availability

| Required Information               | Availability    | Source or Action                           | Intended Use                                 |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Leave types and descriptions       | To Be Confirmed | Obtain approved policy content.            | Answer questions about covered leave types.  |
| Eligibility requirements           | To Be Confirmed | Obtain approved eligibility rules.         | Explain documented eligibility requirements. |
| Leave approval procedures          | To Be Confirmed | Obtain the documented approval process.    | Explain the leave-request process.           |
| Common leave FAQs                  | To Be Confirmed | Collect or create approved FAQ content.    | Answer frequently asked questions.           |
| When to contact HR                 | To Be Confirmed | Confirm documented escalation guidance.    | Direct users to HR when appropriate.         |
| Policy definitions and terminology | To Be Confirmed | Confirm definitions in approved materials. | Maintain consistent explanations.            |
| Policy version and effective date  | To Be Confirmed | Confirm the current approved version.      | Support accurate knowledge maintenance.      |
| Out-of-scope scenarios             | To Be Confirmed | Define scenarios requiring HR review.      | Prevent unsupported guidance or decisions.   |


7. Overall Use Case Assessment

The Employee Leave Policy Assistant is a clearly scoped Custom GPT use case focused on providing information from approved leave-policy content. Implementation should proceed only after the required knowledge sources, policy version, and out-of-scope scenarios are confirmed.
