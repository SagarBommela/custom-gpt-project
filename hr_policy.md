# Artscape HR Policy

## Leave Policy

## Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leave days per year.

Casual leave should be requested through the applicable company leave
process and is subject to manager approval.

---

## Sick Leave

Employees may use sick leave according to the standard company leave policy.

Sick leave follows the standard employee rules.

---
### Leave Approval

Leave requests are subject to the applicable approval process.

Employees should follow the company's designated leave-request procedure and obtain the required approval.

This policy does not provide a separate rule determining whether a particular manager's leave decision is fair or unfair.

---

## Holiday Policy

### Public Holidays

Artscape observes public holidays according to the applicable company holiday calendar.

The holiday calendar determines the dates recognized as public holidays.

This policy does not specify whether employees may work from home on a public holiday.

Questions about working arrangements on a public holiday should be confirmed with HR.

---

## Reimbursement Policy

### Internet Reimbursement

Eligible employees may claim internet expenses up to a maximum reimbursement limit of **₹1,000 per month**.

Reimbursement claims must follow the applicable company reimbursement process.

### Reimbursement Submission

Employees should submit reimbursement claims through the designated reimbursement process with the required supporting information and documentation.

Claims are reviewed according to the applicable reimbursement procedure.

### Rejected Reimbursements

This policy does not specify a separate appeal process for rejected reimbursement claims.

Employees who need clarification about a rejected reimbursement should contact HR for guidance.

## Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager
approval.

WFH requests should normally be communicated at least 1 day in advance,
except in emergencies.

Employees working from home are expected to remain reachable during
core hours:

10:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Work from home does not count against casual leave or sick leave.

New employees during their first 30 days are expected to work from the
office to support onboarding.

---

## Probation Period Leave Rules

Employees in their first 90 days are considered to be in the probation
period.

Probation employees accrue leave but require manager approval to use
accrued leave, except sick leave.

Sick leave follows the standard employee rules.

Unused leave carries over upon confirmation.
